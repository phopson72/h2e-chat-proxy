Motto: Vision guides. People build. Execution wins.

THE PATTERN H2E FIXES
Most operators are stuck in one of three places:
- Every decision runs through the owner, who is buried in daily decisions instead of leading the business forward.
- A talented team pulls in different directions. Meetings do not produce decisions, and accountability is inconsistent.
- Growth without a roadmap. The opportunities are there, but scaling feels risky without an operating plan.
What H2E does about it: takes the seat (workflows, meeting rhythm, decision rights), aligns the leadership team and holds it accountable from inside the business, and builds the roadmap with the client, staying until their people can run it without H2E.

THREE WAYS TO WORK WITH H2E
All three share two things: H2E works inside the business rather than beside it, and every engagement is designed to end in operational independence.

1. Fractional Leader (here2elevate.com/fractional)
- Best for: organizations that need experienced leadership embedded in the team, or newer leaders in the seat who need mentoring alongside it.
- How it works: an executive in a leadership seat on the client's team, working in the business alongside their people. Starts with a diagnostic, then takes the seat, builds the operating structure, hires and develops the people who will own the function, and leaves with a transition plan built in advance.
- Good fit when a senior seat is empty and a search would take six to nine months, the owner is holding a function personally, a capable leader needs an experienced operator beside them, or growth has outrun the structure.
- Commitment: ongoing, typically fifteen to twenty-five hours a week (one to three days a week).
- Investment: monthly retainer based on scope and hours.
- Seats filled: operations, marketing, finance, sales, learning and development, and people.

2. Advisor and Coach (here2elevate.com/advisor)
- Best for: CEOs and leadership teams who want strategic coaching and an operating system that holds between meetings.
- How it works: monthly or quarterly sessions with the leadership team, one-to-one coaching with the CEO, and an operating framework that gives the work somewhere to live between conversations.
- Good fit when the leadership team is not yet operating as one, decisions get made and then quietly do not happen, the client wants an operating system installed by someone who has run one, or wants someone in the room who will disagree with them.
- Commitment: ongoing, monthly or quarterly rhythm.
- Investment: retainer, hourly, or subscription.
- H2E coaches are certified BOS-UP coaches. If the client already runs on an operating system, H2E works inside it.

3. Project Consultant (here2elevate.com/consultant)
- Best for: a specific problem or project that needs expert execution and a defined end.
- How it works: a defined scope with clear deliverables, a start date, and a finish date.
- Good fit when a department needs restructuring, a system or process needs building once and handing over, the client needs capability they will not need permanently, or wants to work with H2E before committing to something larger.
- Commitment: fixed duration, project dependent.
- Investment: project fee or hourly.
- Full access to the H2E bench across operations, marketing, people, finance, learning and development, and technology.

HOW AN ENGAGEMENT GOES (four stages, "built to hand back")
1. Discovery: learn the business, the constraint, and what is actually holding it back.
2. Custom plan: a strategy built to the client's vision rather than a template.
3. Engaged execution: H2E takes the seat. Systems built, leaders coached, accountability held.
4. Sustainable growth: H2E hands it back and steps out, with support as needed.

FRAMEWORKS H2E BRINGS
- BOS-UP: a business operating framework H2E delivers, created by Scott Abbott and powered by Ninety. It aligns vision, customer, goals, people, structure, data, meetings, process, and exit planning. If a client already runs on an operating system, H2E works inside it rather than replacing it.
- Everything DiSC: for self-awareness. How you show up, how others experience you, and how to lead with intention.
- The Five Behaviors: for teams. Trust, healthy conflict, commitment, accountability, and results.
- Learning and development: develop the bench before you need it, investing in everyone, not only people with a title.

THE ELEVATED STANDARD AND THE ELEVATED FRACTIONAL (here2elevate.com/the-elevated-fractional-certification)
- The Elevated Standard defines how the work gets done, from the first assessment to the clean exit, in five disciplines: Assess, Align, Architect, Accelerate, Ascend.
  - Assess: enter with a proven diagnostic lens, and build trust while using it.
  - Align: operating agreements, coaching structure, and clear ownership through RACI.
  - Architect: ninety-day blueprints, named deliverables, and accountability that holds.
  - Accelerate: the execution rhythm that produces maximum impact on limited hours.
  - Ascend: the clean exit, a designed transition that preserves what was built.
- The Elevated Fractional is the certification built on that standard: the only certification of its kind in multifamily. It is not an introductory program. It is for experienced C-suite executives already operating at the fractional level in multifamily and investment real estate.
- Two elevated tracks: Business Development (filling your own pipeline) and Marketing and Positioning (ideal client, personal brand, positioning as the expert).
- Includes a community through the partnership with Fractionals on Deck.
- Founding members include Keith Misner, Mike Boone, and Suzanne Hopson.
- To apply, visitors use the application form linked on that page or the Contact page. A brochure is also available on that page.

Eight questions, ending with a result profile, a coaching interpretation, and three questions worth sitting with. Available on The Elevated Fractional and Contact pages.
